An Edit

Welcome to the Tatari documentation proof of concept (POC) built on Mintlify. This site is a sandbox for evaluating whether Mintlify is the right platform for Tatari's documentation: how content is written and reviewed, how navigation scales, and how readers (and AI tools) find answers.

<Note>
  This is a draft evaluation site. Content here is illustrative and may not reflect current Tatari products or processes.
</Note>

## POC goals

<Steps>
  <Step title="Author content">
    Create and edit pages in the visual editor or in MDX, and confirm the workflow fits the team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize navigation">
    Group pages into sections and tabs that mirror how Tatari users look for information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evaluate the reader experience">
    Test search, the AI assistant, and page feedback from a reader's point of view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish">
    Walk through the review and publishing flow to validate governance needs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Have feedback on the POC? Capture it as comments directly on the page in the editor so the team can review it in context.
</Tip>
