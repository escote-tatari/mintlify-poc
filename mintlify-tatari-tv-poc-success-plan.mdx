## Purpose of this document

This is the shared plan for Tatari TV's evaluation of Mintlify. It records what we're proving, who's involved, what happens in each session, and what Tatari TV will do between sessions. We'll keep it updated as we go and score it together at the end.

## Tatari TV's Objective

Tatari's documentation is fragmented across Confluence, Jira, Google Docs, GitHub, Slack, Simplr, and an in-house tool built to fill the gaps, leaving contributors unsure where content belongs and readers unsure where to look. The core objective is to unify that surface onto a single Git-backed platform and, in doing so, make PRDs and tech specs faster to produce: intelligently retrieving context about new features from across source code repositories and third-party apps, so the burden of assembling and writing these documents is lifted from PMs, engineers, and engineering
